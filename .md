# Weather and Socioeconomic Factors Associated with Antidepressant Prescribing: England vs Spain (2021–2025)


### Research Question

To what extent are weather and socioeconomic factors associated with antidepressant prescribing patterns in England and Spain between 2021 and 2025?

### Objectives

* Compare antidepressant and anxiolytic prescribing trends.
* Investigate relationships between sunshine, precipitation, and prescribing rates.
* Compare prescribing patterns between England and Spain.


This project investigates whether weather conditions and socioeconomic factors are associated with antidepressant prescribing rates and whether these relationships differ between England and Spain.
